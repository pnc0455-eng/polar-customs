Frequently Asked Questions

What are the VIP Subscriptions for?

The VIP Subscriptions are specifically for access to our automated **Xbox Follower Bot**. Subscribing unlocks Discord commands that let you boost followers on Xbox accounts instantly. It is completely separate from custom modded account purchases.

Why do I need to join the Discord server to request a custom account?

Our backend uses an automated bot to send your custom payment link directly to your Discord DMs once staff approves your build options. If you are not in our server, the bot cannot message you!

How long does custom account delivery take?

Due to the complexity of custom builds, fulfillment takes between **24 to 48 hours** after payment is verified. You can check the live progress of your build using the Order Tracker on this site. (Pre-made accounts are delivered instantly).

How does Ban Insurance work?

If you add the $10 Ban Insurance to your Custom Account order, we guarantee a free replacement account if your account is banned within 30 days of delivery. Just open a ticket in our Discord!
